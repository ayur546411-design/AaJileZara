Frequently Asked Questions

Is this counselling or therapy?

No. Ji Le Zara provides coaching and mentoring for personal development; it does not diagnose or treat medical or mental-health conditions.

Are sessions online?

Yes. Sessions are intended to be conducted by video or audio appointment.

Will my session be recorded?

Sessions are intended to be recorded only after explicit consent from all participants. Recordings are planned to be retained for 90 days after the final session, unless a different legal retention obligation applies. Please review the recording policy before booking.

How do I book and pay?

Use the booking enquiry form to request a session. Confirmed time slots and payment instructions are provided separately until the live booking and payment systems are activated.
